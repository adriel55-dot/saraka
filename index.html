Welcome to Saraka

Saraka is a traditional Grenadian custom associated with community, sharing and cultural heritage. Food plays an important role in bringing people together and keeping traditions alive.

This website provides information about Saraka, the cooking competition, participation requirements and the rules contestants should follow.
