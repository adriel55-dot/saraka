Rules and Regulations

Fair play, food safety, teamwork and respect.

All participants should follow the official competition requirements provided by the organisers. The general guidelines below are suggestions and must be confirmed before being presented as official event rules.

01

Registration

Teams must register using the official method and meet the organisers' entry requirements.

02

Arrival and Preparation

Participants should arrive at the confirmed time and prepare their cooking area as instructed.

03

Food Hygiene

Wash hands, keep utensils clean, use safe ingredients and prevent cross-contamination.

04

Cooking Safety

Handle knives, hot pots, open flames and other cooking equipment carefully. Follow the safety instructions provided.

05

Teamwork and Conduct

Teams must treat judges, organisers, other participants and spectators with respect.

06

Ingredients and Equipment

Follow the organisers' instructions regarding permitted ingredients, equipment and cooking methods.

07

Judging

Judging categories and scoring must be announced by the organisers before the competition.

08

Fair Play

Cheating, interfering with other teams or disregarding safety instructions may result in penalties under the official rules.

09

Final Decisions

Participants should follow the official procedures for questions, disputes and decisions made by the judges.

Before You Compete

Contact the event organisers to confirm the official rules, scoring system, permitted ingredients, safety requirements and any disqualification procedures.
